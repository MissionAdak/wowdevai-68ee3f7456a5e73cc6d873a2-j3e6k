PM-AJAY Components

Three key initiatives for scheduled tribe welfare and development

Adarsh Gram

Model village development focusing on infrastructure, education, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities for scheduled tribes.

Grant-in-Aid (GIA)

Financial support for NGOs and voluntary organizations working on scheduled tribe development projects and initiatives.

Hostel Facilities

Residential accommodation facilities for scheduled tribe students to support their educational journey and reduce dropout rates.
